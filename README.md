## Project Overview
Wildflower & Wax is a boutique, home-based artisanal candle and wellness brand originally founded in 2024. This project involves building a clean, responsive, and intuitive e-commerce platform to showcase sustainable, eco-friendly home fragrances, shifting the brand's presence from physical local market stalls to a nationwide digital marketplace.

## Website Goals and Objectives
- **Primary Goal:** Establish an intuitive e-commerce framework to showcase and sell boutique items directly online.
- **KPI 1:** Reach 1,500 monthly unique visitors within the first six months of launch.
- **KPI 2:** Achieve a minimum 3% visitor-to-purchase conversion rate.
- **KPI 3:** Secure 200 newsletter club subscriptions during the initial launch month.

## Key Features and Functionality
- Semantic HTML5 structure for optimal multi-device navigation.
- Dedicated product presentation grid showcasing 4 selective artisanal candle lines.
- Context-specific Enquiry form catering to bulk order and stockist requests.
- Multi-location contact matrix displaying local Pretoria pickup destination maps.
